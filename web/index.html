About & disclaimer

  • Unofficial. This is a personal project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or maintained by Northeastern University. University names are used only to describe locations.
  • “Free” only means no class is scheduled. The data comes from the public course registration “Browse Classes” page. It includes scheduled classes and final exams only. It does not include events, meetings, office hours, room reservations, cancellations, make-up classes, or building hours. Holidays and breaks are added by hand from the academic calendar, which says its dates are subject to change.
  • Rooms may be locked, reserved, or occupied. Follow posted signs, building access rules, and university policies. If a room is in use, leave it for the people using it.
  • Only rooms that host classes appear. Rooms with no scheduled classes this term are not listed. “Up to N” is the largest class scheduled in the room, not its official capacity.
  • No guarantees. The schedule may be out of date or wrong. Use at your own risk.
  • Privacy. This site has no accounts, cookies, analytics, or trackers, and it loads no third-party resources. All filtering happens in your browser.
